# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing leads own quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They collaborate with developers and product teams to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans aligned to features and acceptance criteria
- Design and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end tests
- Validate features against acceptance criteria
- Identify and triage quality issues
- Contribute to Definition of Done and quality metrics

### Goals
- Ensure production releases meet quality and reliability standards
- Reduce defect escape rates and improve customer experience
- Enable rapid, confident iteration

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and refinement sessions
- Quality status in weekly delivery syncs
- Test result reports and blockers in standups

---

## Scrum Master / Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters (or Agile Facilitators) remove impediments, coach teams on agile practices, and facilitate ceremonies. They enable teams to self-organize and deliver value consistently.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and escalate impediments
- Coach team on agile principles and continuous improvement
- Maintain team velocity and health metrics
- Support sprint planning and capacity planning

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and delivery cadence
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning
- Enable sustainable pace and reduce burnout

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members for blockers
- Metrics and retrospective action items

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide business context, secure resources, and remove organizational barriers. They champion projects and ensure alignment with strategic goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and strategic alignment
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Escalate cross-functional dependencies and organizational blockers
- Attend key milestones and reviews
- Communicate project outcomes to executive leadership

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Remove organizational blockers quickly
- Align project outcomes with strategic priorities

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and executive summaries
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Escalation and decision approval (as needed)

---

## Security Officer / Security Champion

### Role Summary
Security Officers ensure compliance, threat modeling, and secure practices across the project. They collaborate with engineering to integrate security early and often.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct threat modeling and security reviews
- Define security acceptance criteria and compliance requirements
- Review code and architecture for security risks
- Support incident response and post-incident analysis
- Advise on secure deployment and data handling

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities and data breaches
- Enable secure-by-default development practices
- Maintain compliance with organizational and regulatory standards

### Typical Communication
- Design and code reviews for security implications
- Security incident response coordination
- Quarterly security updates and training

---

## Technical Architect / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define system design, technical strategy, and ensure scalability, maintainability, and performance. They guide technical decisions and coach the team on best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Define system architecture and technical direction
- Review designs for scalability, performance, and maintainability
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Guide technology selections and trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant systems
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risk
- Enable consistent, high-quality engineering practices

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and mentoring
- Technical risk assessment in planning and retrospectives
